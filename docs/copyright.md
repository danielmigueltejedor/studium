# Copyright

Providing a file does not make it open, licensed for the book, or safe to copy. Rights stay `unknown` until a later rights review. Exams, campus slides, and unpublished notes stay private.

Local material may inform skills, scope, terminology, and emphasis. It must not be copied into exercises or chapters. `do_not_copy_protected_sources` remains the default. A release manifest records safe source metadata, not the protected file.
