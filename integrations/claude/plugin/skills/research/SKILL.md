---
name: research
description: Research an academic topic for Studium using reliable legally reusable sources with provenance and independent corroboration.
---

Use Studium's full MCP catalog, not a curated subset: inspect `studium_server_info` if tools are missing. The server never magically browses the web; first open any needed public pages using your actual available client tools, then register bibliographic metadata and excerpts in Studium. Do not claim to have opened sources that the client cannot access.

Delegate bibliographic discovery to `bibliography-researcher` and substantive review to the appropriate `subject-specialist` or `discipline-evidence-reviewer`. Distinguish source provenance from endorsement of scientific or historical assertions; explicitly label conflicts and unsupported claims.

Record source IDs and citation details; avoid importing copyrighted or unauthorized copies. Do not expose personal files or search folders without the user's authorization. Hand back evidence coverage, quality limitations and next research tasks, usable by the book or new-edition skill.
